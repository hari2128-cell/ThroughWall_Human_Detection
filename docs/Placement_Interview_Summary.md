# Placement / Interview Summary (2-Minute Version)

"My project, **Through-Wall Human Motion Detection**, uses a microwave Doppler
radar sensor and an ESP32 to detect human movement behind non-metallic
obstacles. The radar transmits microwave signals, and any movement introduces
a Doppler frequency shift in the reflected waves. The ESP32 acquires the
radar's analog output through its ADC and streams the sampled data to a
computer over UART. MATLAB performs digital signal processing, including
noise removal, filtering, FFT-based frequency analysis, and threshold-based
motion detection. The processed data is visualized using real-time waveforms,
frequency spectra, and heatmaps, allowing us to observe motion patterns
clearly. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience in embedded
systems, signal acquisition, digital signal processing, serial communication,
MATLAB-based analysis, and technical documentation. This project strengthened
my understanding of integrating hardware with signal processing algorithms to
build practical sensing systems."
